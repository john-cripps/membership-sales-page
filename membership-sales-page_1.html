What Is The Official Cricket Widow Clubhouse?

This is a place for cricket widows across the country (and the world) to come together in a way that just isn't possible when we open the doors to everyone.

A private space for real cricket widows who live this life daily and want to share it with their peers… and, of course, with me too!

Because this group is so much more than another social media platform. It's an intimate, shared space where everyone can chat in real time — not just comment on a post and wait.

Private Server

Okay — I said the Survival Guide was the best bonus. I take it back.

By far, the most valuable thing I can give you is the exact reason I started this page in the first place: a community of women who get it.

So what makes our private server different from our Skool group? Skool is brilliant for posting your thoughts and sharing comments — but it's still a feed. You post something, and you wait for someone to reply.

Our private server doesn't work like that. It's a running conversation, not a noticeboard — more like a group chat than a comments section. I'm in there most days, so instead of posting a thought and waiting to see if anyone responds, you can just talk. To me, properly, whenever you need to. To the other cricket widows in real time, the second something happens — good or bad. No waiting for someone to see your post. No performing for a feed. Just a direct line into the community, exactly when you need it.

That's the whole point of this club. A place that's actually there for you, not just a place you post to.
